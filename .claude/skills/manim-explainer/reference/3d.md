# 3D scenes

Base: `class _Base3D(Explainer3DScene)`, or a shared mixin
`class _Common(ExplainerMixin)` combined with `Scene` and `ThreeDScene` when one film
mixes 2D and 3D scenes (Backpropagation, CrossEntropy, Momentum, Ptolemy). A single
Scene class cannot be both, so such films render `full` by stitching: declare
`FILM=""` in `render.sh` and list the scenes in `SCENES`.

## When to move the camera
Only when there is real 3D geometry to see whole: a loss surface, a weight field, an
embedding manifold, crates on a host. Tilt to a 3/4 view and orbit slowly
(`begin_ambient_camera_rotation` or a `theta` `ValueTracker`). **Never zoom or pan a 2D
scene** (formulas, charts, text): a push-in on flat content adds nothing and the user
called it out. 2D motion comes from the mobjects (Write, Transform, GrowFromEdge, a dot
riding a curve, Indicate/Circumscribe).

## Cairo 3D has no lighting
A `Prism`/`Cube` in one colour reads as a dark blob. Build boxes from per-face shaded
`Polygon`s (bright roof, lit sides, shadow sides) with a visible edge stroke; painter's
sort handles occlusion. Keep the subject low enough that labels stay readable; add glows
after the geometry so they draw on top.

## Fixed-in-frame HUD (captions, headers, legends)
- Pin with `self.hud(m)` (= `add_fixed_in_frame_mobjects`) and position in 2D frame
  coordinates (`to_corner`, `to_edge`).
- To animate a pinned mobject in: `self.add_fixed_in_frame_mobjects(m); self.remove(m);
  self.play(FadeIn(m))`. Adding then removing renders no frame and keeps it fixed.
- Rebuilding glyphs (e.g. `DecimalNumber.set_value`) creates submobjects that are *not*
  fixed: they get projected into 3D. Re-pin the **parent** group after each update
  (re-pinning children pulls them out of the panel and breaks a later `FadeOut(panel)`).
- Size HUD boxes to wrap their content; make them opaque with a bright border so they
  survive a dark 3D background.

## A stage with its own draw order (`animations/Terraform/stage.py`)
Manim only depth-sorts `shade_in_3d` mobjects, and by the centre of each mobject. A
floor polygon is then drawn over a box standing on its far half, and a glass wall
tints what is behind it at random. When a scene is a *set* (a floor, plates, objects
standing on them), give the camera the order instead:

- `StageCamera(ThreeDCamera)`: every face carries `stage = (layer, order)`, an
  `anchor` (the base of the object it belongs to) and a `normal`. The camera draws the
  layers in order (floor, plates, solids), sorts solids by the depth of their *object*
  (all faces of a block share the anchor), skips faces that look away from the camera
  (a convex block then needs no sorting inside), and puts the near walls of a glass
  enclosure after its content and the far walls before (`bias`).
- `screen_shift` is added after projection: the subject sits right of a HUD column
  while the camera still turns around the subject, not around the frame centre.
- `hud_roots`: groups whose whole *current* family is fixed in the frame on every
  capture, so panels whose updaters add or rebuild children stay on screen.
- `frame(stages, zone)` computes zoom and screen position from the projected corners
  of the set over the whole camera move, so it fills a zone of the screen at every angle.
- Sway (`theta = theta0 + A sin(t)`) instead of a full orbit when the set has a front:
  the HUD stays readable, floor lettering stays the right way up.
- Billboards: `add_fixed_orientation_mobjects` projects the mobject's *own* centre.
  Keep a label on a moving anchor with an updater (`m.move_to(anchor())`), not with
  `center_func` alone. A billboard is drawn at its own size whatever the zoom: budget
  it in screen units (shrink the zone by half its height on the side it hangs from),
  not in world units, and frame the set on what it really reaches (`frame(grow=1.0)`
  when nothing grows): room reserved for nothing costs zoom on everything else.
- Parts are attribute-driven (`rise`, `size`, `alpha`, `tint` animated with `Tween`),
  shaded once from their normals; a *ghost* (glass block) and a *solid* of the same
  resource show "declared" against "real".

## Examples
`animations/Terraform` (a diorama with its own camera, HUD column, 2D and 3D scenes),
`animations/Quantization` (orbiting weight-bar field, first `ThreeDScene` of the repo),
